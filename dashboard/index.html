How the radar values a launch

An implied value is the market cap a protocol's token would carry if its record stayed as it is. The radar derives it from the snapshot's own figures, never from a list of names:

implied value = TVL × peer multiple × momentum × track record × product factor

  • Peer multiple. For each family of protocols, the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the ones whose token already trades (the launched set below). A lending market is priced against launched lending markets, not against the whole field.
  • Momentum. The protocol's seven-day TVL change, clamped: +80% or more earns ×1.32, −40% or worse earns ×0.84, no report earns ×1.00.
  • Track record. How long it has run with no token: ×0.80 on day one, rising to ×1.20 at three years. An unreported start date earns ×0.90.
  • Product factor. A wrapped, staked or bridged product of an asset that already trades — or an exchange — has no launch of its own to price: its figure is a claim on the underlying asset, discounted to ×0.03 and flagged wrapped on the board. Native protocols keep ×1.00.
